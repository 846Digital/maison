Режим: встроенный Imagegen, генерация нового изображения по тексту, без фотографии человека и без стороннего референса в генераторе.

Финальный веб-ассет: dist/assets/paper-texture.webp (1536 × 1024).
Автономная копия: sites/04-paper/assets/paper-texture.webp.

Финальный промпт:

> Generate a premium photorealistic material texture asset for a gentle handmade wedding invitation website. A single blank sheet of ivory warm off-white handmade cotton paper, gently crumpled and flattened again, shallow irregular creases, delicate visible cellulose fibres and realistic paper grain. Orthographic perfectly flat top-down scan, fills the entire 1536x1024 landscape image edge to edge, no torn edge visible. Soft broad diffuse natural light, almost uniform warm cream colour, subtle dimensional crease shadows, no dramatic lighting. Medium subtle visible wrinkles across the full surface, no vignette, no writing, absolutely no letters, no objects, no flowers, no people, no border or UI. Needs to remain a readable website background behind dark brown text, archival tactile paper aesthetic.
